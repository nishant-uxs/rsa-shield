Cryptographic Foundation

Why Elliptic Curve Method?

Lenstra ECM is an established factorization algorithm — useful for finding relatively small factors of large composites. We apply it as an audit instrument, not as a claimed break of modern RSA.

flowchart TD
  N[RSA modulus n] --> S[ECM search]
  S --> E[Elliptic curve operations]
  E --> Q{Non-trivial factor?}
  Q -->|YES| Y[Factor found — concrete weakness evidence]
  Q -->|NO| X[Continue / Report inconclusive]

What ECM is A factorization method particularly effective when a composite has a relatively small prime factor.
Why it matters for audit Discovering a non-trivial factor of n is concrete evidence the modulus is mathematically weak.
What we do not claim ECM does not instantly factor properly generated modern RSA keys.

Responsible Cryptography

What RSA-SHIELD Does — and Does Not — Claim

What it does

  • Audits authorized RSA public keys and moduli
  • Applies structural and mathematical analysis
  • Uses Lenstra ECM as an evidence-gathering probe
  • Classifies findings with explicit evidence tiers
  • Produces human-readable security assessments

What it does not claim

  • ECM is not a magic attack on modern strong RSA
  • Failure to factor ≠ mathematical proof of security
  • Not a formal cryptographic security proof
  • Production use needs specialist validation
  • Only authorized keys / infrastructure may be audited
Credibility comes from precision: we operationalize established math into an auditing aid — we do not invent RSA, ECM, or a new cryptosystem.
